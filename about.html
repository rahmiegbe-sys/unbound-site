The Story

Built on a wall

UNBOUND ORIGIN FILE LAGOS · DROP 001

Unbound didn't start in a studio. It started on concrete — the same walls, gates, and danfo panels that raised a generation of Lagos teens on colour, tags, and noise.

Unbound was never meant to be just a clothing brand. It's a canvas that walked off the wall and onto the street.

The mind behind it grew up chasing graffiti and street art before he ever thought about stitching — tagging where he could, sketching where he couldn't, obsessed with the idea that a wall, a jacket, or a body could all carry the same message. That obsession is the whole brand.

The ambition was never to sell hoodies. It was to build a lifestyle brand — one where music, art, fashion, and the chaos of being a teenager in Lagos all live in the same universe. Clothing is just the entry point.

Every piece under Unbound carries that same idea: imagination without limitations. No rulebook, no dress code, no permission needed. If you can picture it, you're already unbound.

This isn't merch. It's a creative movement — built by teens, for teens, out of the same spray cans, freestyles, and late-night sketches that make Lagos street culture what it is.
